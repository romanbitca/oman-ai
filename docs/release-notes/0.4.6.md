OmanAI has a new icon, and now installs from a disk image.

- **Quick fix.** Turn it on under Proofread selection in Settings, Shortcuts, and Proofread's shortcut puts the corrections straight back over your selection, without opening the panel. The wave in the menu bar shows a check mark when it is done, and ⌘Z in the app brings back what you wrote.
- **Your own actions.** Save an instruction such as "Make it shorter" or "Turn into bullet points" in Settings, Actions, and run it from the panel or with a shortcut of its own. Its result can replace your selection, as Rewrite's does, or only be shown, as Explain's answer is.
- **A new icon:** the wave from the menu bar, on blue.
- **A disk image to install from.** Open `OmanAI.dmg` and drag OmanAI onto Applications.
- Times in OmanAI's window fit on one line with a 12-hour clock.
